Cosmic Conflict

Procedural game geometry, live HUD graphics, shaders and synthesized sound
effects were created for this project. The cockpit and gun-upgrade bitmaps were
supplied by the project owner for integration; rights to those images remain
with their creator.
The project owner confirms that these images may be redistributed with the game.
No recordings, music, or models from the historical reference games are included.

Recorded sound effects
Water Splash on Cement Series (YouTube Audio Library) — organic destruction
Gun Powder Ignite — mechanical and stone destruction
One effect was excerpted from each supplied recording.

Computer dialogue combines text supplied by the project owner with original
additional lines. The offline recordings were synthesized locally using the
Microsoft Zira Desktop voice, with in-game electronic/radio processing.
The Windows speech engine and voice-engine binaries are not redistributed.
Review all platform/tool terms before wider commercial distribution.
